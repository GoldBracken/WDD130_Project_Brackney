## How did you decide what content and images to use for your site plan?
When I was deciding what content and images to use, I wanted to focus on the purpose of the site. All the images and 
content should in some way support the overall message of the site. For instance, images should be relevant to the 
content message that is shared, which should also in some way relate to the sites main purpose of helping a user with
finding a job or internship. 

## How did you communicate with your developer? Was there any issues with communication?
Early on, my developer and I established that email was the preffered method of communication. However, that
meant that sometimes communication would be slow to be seen, and it was hard to confirm if they had seen the 
email unless they responded.
