## How did you decide what content and images to use for your site plan?
When I was deciding what content and images to use, I wanted to focus on the purpose of the site. All the images and 
content should in some way support the overall message of the site. For instance, images should be relevant to the 
content message that is shared, which should also in some way relate to the sites main purpose of helping a user with
finding a job or internship. 

## How did you communicate with your developer? Was there any issues with communication?
Early on, my developer and I established that email was the preffered method of communication. However, that
meant that sometimes communication would be slow to be seen, and it was hard to confirm if they had seen the 
email unless they responded. This presented a small issue when I noticed the site was fairly lacking in
content and CSS, along with most of the links being broken in some capacity. To ensure that this issue was
addressed I eventually discussed it in person with the lead developer since I could not determine if he had
even seen my email sent days prior.

## Did your developer meet your expectations for your site?
At the time of writing this the answer would unfortunately be no. There are a couple pages that were almost 
entirely what I asked for, and I appreciate those that put in the effort to follow the site plan so exactly. 
However, two pages are essentially deviod of content, which is very disappointing. If there was a reason behind
the missing pages, I was never informed of whether it just was neglected, or the person who was supposed to create
the pages never did them, but in the end the fact that the home page is blank is rather saddening. Also discouraging 
was the fact that despite my direct intervention with the broken links and href and discussing them with the lead 
developer, almost none of the images are working properly at this time.

## What will you do differently next time you hire a team to do a project for you?
I think somethings I would do differently next time I hire a team to do a project is look at their previous
work to see if they make good websites and use a different method of communication. The communication method
I would use would be faster, easier to check, and have more fluid communication design, like discord or google 
chat, where direct messages could quickly be seen and responded to.
